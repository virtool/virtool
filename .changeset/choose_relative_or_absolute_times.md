Choose relative or absolute times

You can now show times as a date and time, such as "Today at 11:12" or "Sep 26 at 14:03", instead of a relative time, such as "3 days ago". Times from an earlier year show the date only, such as "Sep 26, 2023". Go to **Account** > **Settings** and set **Time format**. To see a time in the other format, point to it, select it with the keyboard, or tap it.
