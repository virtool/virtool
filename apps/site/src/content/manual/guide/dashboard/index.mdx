## Overview

The dashboard is the first page that you see after you sign in. It shows two cards:

- **Recent Samples**
- **Recent Analyses**

To go back to the dashboard from a different page, click the Virtool logo at the left
of the navigation bar.

## Recent Samples

This card shows up to 10 samples. Use the toggle in the card header to select the
samples that it shows:

- **Viewed** shows the samples that you opened, with the most recent first. This is
  the default.
- **Created** shows the samples that you created, with the most recent first.

Each row shows the sample name, the analysis workflows of the sample, the user who
created the sample, and when the sample was created. Click the sample name to open the
sample.

If there are more than 10 samples, the last row tells you how many samples the card
does not show. On the **Created** tab, click this row to open the
[Samples](/docs/guide/samples) list with only your samples.

The **Viewed** tab shows only the samples that you can read now. If you lose access to
a sample, it is removed from the card.

## Recent Analyses

This card shows up to 10 analyses from all samples. Use the toggle in the card header
to select the analyses that it shows:

- **Viewed** shows the analyses that you opened, with the most recent first. This is
  the default.
- **Created** shows the analyses that you started, with the most recent first.

Each row shows the workflow, the workflow version, the sample, the user who started the
analysis, and when the analysis was created. Click the workflow name to open the
analysis. Click the sample name to open the sample. If Virtool can't show results for
the workflow, the workflow name is not a link.

If there are more than 10 analyses, the last row tells you how many analyses the card
does not show. Virtool has no list of all analyses. To find an analysis that is not on
the card, open its sample. See [Analyses](/docs/guide/analyses).

## When a card is empty

A card is empty until you open or create a sample or an analysis. When you open a
sample or an analysis, it is added to the **Viewed** tab.

## Keep your selection

The selected tab of each card is part of the page address. To go back to the same
selection, bookmark the dashboard or share its address.
