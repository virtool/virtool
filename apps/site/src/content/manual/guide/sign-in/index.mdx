## Overview

You must sign in before you can use Virtool. This page tells you about the screens that
you see before you are in the app:

- The invitation page, where you set up a new account.
- The sign-in page.
- The steps that your Virtool instance can ask you to complete after you sign in.
- The pages that open when you click a recovery or verification link.

To change your account settings after you sign in, see [Account](/docs/guide/account).

## Accept an invitation

An administrator creates your account and gives you an invitation link. You get the link
in an email with the subject **Set up your Virtool account**, or the administrator sends
it to you.

1. Open the invitation link.
2. Under **Username**, type a username.
3. Under **Password**, type a password.
4. Under **Confirm password**, type the password again.
5. Click **Create account**.

The page shows the email address of the invitation and the date when the link expires.
The link works one time. It expires 72 hours after the administrator creates it.

Your username is also your handle. It must have 3 to 30 characters. It can contain only
letters, numbers, underscores (`_`), and periods (`.`). You can't use the username
`virtool` or a username that another account uses.

By default, your password must contain at least 8 characters. An administrator can set a
different minimum.

When you click **Create account**, Virtool signs you in. Then it does one of these
actions:

- It opens Virtool.
- It shows a required step. See
  [Required steps after sign-in](#required-steps-after-sign-in).
- It shows **Check your email**. Virtool sends a verification link to your address.
  Click **Continue to Virtool**. You can open the link later. See
  [Verify or change an email address](#verify-or-change-an-email-address).

### If the invitation link does not work

If the link expired, was replaced, or was used, the page shows **Invitation
unavailable**. Ask an administrator to
[reissue the invitation](/docs/guide/users#reissue-an-invitation). Use the new link. The
old link does not work again.

## Sign in

You can sign in with your password or with a passkey.

### Sign in with your password

1. Under **Username**, type your username.
2. Under **Password**, type your password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

If your account uses an authenticator app, Virtool asks for a code. See
[Enter an authenticator code](#enter-an-authenticator-code).

If you type an incorrect password too many times, Virtool shows **Too many sign-in
attempts. Wait a moment and try again.** Wait, then try again.

### Sign in with a passkey

1. Click **Sign in with a passkey**.
2. Follow the instructions from your browser or device.

You can also click the **Username** field. Your browser can show your passkeys there.

When you sign in with a passkey, Virtool does not ask for an authenticator code.

Passkeys need a supported browser and a secure (HTTPS) connection. If your browser can't
use passkeys, the page shows **Passkey sign-in is not available in this browser.** Sign
in with your password.

To add a passkey to your account, see [Passkeys](/docs/guide/account#passkeys).

### Enter an authenticator code

If you set up
[two-factor authentication](/docs/guide/account#two-factor-authentication), Virtool
shows **Two-factor authentication** after you enter your password.

1. Open your authenticator app.
2. Type the 6-digit code that the app shows for Virtool.
3. Click **Verify**.

If you can't use your authenticator app, use a recovery code:

1. Click **Use recovery code**.
2. Type one of your recovery codes.
3. Click **Verify**.

Each recovery code works one time. To go back to the code from your app, click **Use
authenticator code**. To start again, click **Back to sign in**.

You have 10 minutes to enter a code. After 5 incorrect codes, you must sign in again. If
you enter too many incorrect codes, Virtool locks the code step for 15 minutes and shows
**Too many incorrect codes. Wait a few minutes, then sign in again.**

If you lost your authenticator app and your recovery codes, ask a full administrator to
[reset your two-factor authentication](/docs/guide/users#reset-two-factor-authentication).

### When your session ends

A session ends after 7 days without use. An administrator or a password change can also
end it. Then Virtool shows **Your session ended. Sign in again.** Sign in again to
continue.

## Required steps after sign-in

Virtool can ask you to complete a step before you can use the app. You see these steps
in this order:

1. **Add your email**, if your account does not have a unique email address.
2. **Set up two-factor authentication**, if your Virtool instance requires it.

If you do not need to complete a step, Virtool does not show it.

### Add your email

Some accounts do not have a unique email address. When you sign in to one of these
accounts, Virtool shows **Add your email**.

1. Under **Email address**, type your email address.
2. Click **Continue**.

If email delivery is not set up for your Virtool instance, Virtool accepts the address
and continues. It does not send a verification link.

If email delivery is set up, Virtool shows **Check your email** and sends a verification
link to the address. The link is valid for 72 hours. Open it in any browser.

- If you open the link in the same browser, Virtool shows **Email verified**. Click
  **Continue**.
- If you open the link in a different browser, Virtool shows **Email verified** and
  **Sign in**. In the first browser, the page changes to **Email verified**. Click
  **Continue** there.

On the **Check your email** page, you can also do these actions:

- Click **Resend** to send a new link. You must wait 60 seconds between messages. The
  old link stops working.
- Click **Change email** to type a different address. The old link stops working.
- Click **Cancel** to sign out.

Another account can't use the same address. If it does, Virtool shows **Another account
uses this email address.**

You must add your address within 30 minutes after you sign in. If you do not, Virtool
shows **Your email setup expired. Sign in again to continue.**

### Set up two-factor authentication

If your Virtool instance requires two-factor authentication, you must set it up before
you can use Virtool. Virtool shows this step if your account has no authenticator app
and no passkey.

1. Type your password, then click **Continue**.
2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or enter the setup key.
3. Under **Code**, type the code that the app shows, then click **Turn on**.
4. Save your recovery codes. Click **Copy all** or **Download**.
5. Select **I have saved these codes**, then click **Done**.

You see the recovery codes only once. To leave before you turn on two-factor
authentication, click **Cancel and sign out**.

A passkey also meets this requirement. If you add a passkey, you do not need an
authenticator app.

Virtool shows this step again if you turn off two-factor authentication and you have no
passkey. It also shows this step if you remove your last passkey and you have no
authenticator app.

## Forgot your password?

If email delivery is set up and your email address is verified, you can request a link
to choose a new password.

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot your password?**.
2. Under **Username**, type your username.
3. Click **Send recovery link**.

Virtool shows **If this account can receive recovery email, a link has been queued.**
Virtool shows this message for all usernames. It does not tell you if the account exists
or if it sent an email.

Look for an email with the subject **Reset your Virtool password**. If you requested a
link in the last hour, Virtool does not send a new link. Use the link that you have.

If you do not get an email, ask an administrator to
[create a reset link](/docs/guide/users#help-a-user-recover-access).

### Choose a new password

1. Open the recovery link.
2. Under **New password**, type your new password.
3. Under **Confirm new password**, type the password again.
4. Click **Change password**.

The link works one time. A link that Virtool sends by email expires 7 hours after
Virtool creates it. Your new password must be different from your current password.

When your password changes, Virtool signs out all your browsers. Your other recovery and
verification links stop working. If email delivery is set up, Virtool sends an email
with the subject **Your Virtool password was changed**.

Virtool shows **Password changed**. Click **Sign in**, then sign in with your new
password. Virtool can show a [required step](#required-steps-after-sign-in) after you
sign in.

## Verify or change an email address

Virtool sends a verification link when you
[change your email](/docs/guide/account#change-email). It can also send one when you
accept an invitation. The email has the subject **Verify your email address**.

A verification link is valid for 24 hours. It works one time. When Virtool sends a new
link, the previous link stops working.

### Verify your current address

Open the link. You do not need to sign in. Virtool shows **Email verified**. Click
**Continue to sign in**.

### Change your address

The link completes the change only for the account that requested it.

- If you are signed in to that account, open the link. Virtool shows **Email verified**.
- If you are not signed in, Virtool shows **Sign in to change your email**. Click **Sign
  in**, then sign in to the account that requested the change. The change completes
  after you sign in. Use the same browser tab.

When the change completes, Virtool sends a notice to your old address. Your recovery
links stop working.

## When a link does not work

| Message                                                                                                                 | What to do                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Invitation unavailable**                                                                                              | Ask an administrator to [reissue the invitation](/docs/guide/users#reissue-an-invitation).                                      |
| **Recovery link unavailable**                                                                                           | Click **Request another link**, or ask an administrator to [create a reset link](/docs/guide/users#help-a-user-recover-access). |
| **Verification link unavailable** after you change your email                                                           | Go to **Security** in your [account](/docs/guide/account#change-email) and send a new link.                                     |
| **This verification link has expired.** or **A newer verification link has replaced this one.** when you add your email | Click **Return to email setup** or **Sign in**, then send a new link.                                                           |
| **Verification interrupted**                                                                                            | Click **Try again**. If you are changing your address, sign in to that account first, then open the link again.                 |
| **Virtool is temporarily unavailable**                                                                                  | Your link is not used. Wait one minute, then click **Try again**.                                                               |
