## Overview

To open your account, click your avatar at the right of the main navigation bar, then
click **Account**.

The account pages are in the sidebar:

- **Profile**: your username, avatar, administrator role, and groups.
- **Security**: your email address, password, two-factor authentication, passkeys, and
  sessions.
- **Settings**: your preferences for times, Pathoscope, and reference exports.
- **API Keys**: keys for access to the Virtool API.

To sign in, accept an invitation, or recover a forgotten password, see
[Sign in](/docs/guide/sign-in).

## Change your username and avatar

Your username is the name that other users see. To change it, go to **Profile**, type a
new username, and click **Change**. Virtool keeps the username in lowercase. Your
username is also the name that you sign in with, so Virtool can ask you to confirm your
identity before it changes the username.

Your avatar shows your initials in a circle. To show your Gravatar, turn on **Use
Gravatar**.

## Change email

To change your email address:

1. Go to **Security**.
2. Type the new address under **Email**, then click **Send verification**.
3. Open the verification link that Virtool sends to the new address.

Your current address stays active until you open the link.

**Security** shows **Verified** or **Not verified** beside your address. To verify your
current address, type the same address and click **Send verification**. Password
recovery by email works only for a verified address.

If email delivery is not set up for your Virtool instance, you can't add or change your
address. Ask an administrator to set up
[email delivery](/docs/guide/administration#email-delivery).

## Change password

To change your password:

1. Go to **Security**.
2. Under **Password**, click **Change**.
3. Type your current password and your new password, then click **Change**.

Virtool can ask you to confirm your identity before it changes the password.

Other browsers are signed out when your password changes.

By default, new passwords must contain at least 8 characters.

## Two-factor authentication

With two-factor authentication, you enter a code from an authenticator app when you sign
in with your password. See
[Enter an authenticator code](/docs/guide/sign-in#enter-an-authenticator-code).

### Set up two-factor authentication

1. Go to **Security**.
2. Under **Two-factor authentication**, click **Set up**.
3. Enter your password.
4. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or enter the setup key.
5. Enter the code that the app shows.
6. Save your recovery codes. Click **Copy all** or **Download**.
7. Select **I have saved these codes**, then click **Done**.

You see the recovery codes only once. Each code signs you in one time if you can't use
your authenticator app.

### Make new recovery codes

Click **Make new recovery codes** and enter your password. Your old recovery codes stop
working.

Virtool shows a warning when you have 3 or fewer recovery codes left.

### Turn off two-factor authentication

Click **Turn off** and enter your password. Your authenticator app and recovery codes
stop working.

If your Virtool instance
[requires two-factor authentication](/docs/guide/administration#security), you must set
it up again before you can use Virtool.

## Passkeys

A passkey lets you [sign in](/docs/guide/sign-in#sign-in-with-a-passkey) with your
fingerprint, face, or device PIN. You can always sign in with your password too.

To add a passkey:

1. Go to **Security**.
2. Under **Passkeys**, click **Add**.
3. Follow the instructions from your browser or device.

Virtool can ask you to confirm your identity first. If it does, click **Add** again
after you confirm.

Passkeys need a supported browser and a secure (HTTPS) connection.

You can rename or remove a passkey in your passkey list.

## Sessions

Go to **Security** to see the browsers that are signed in to your account. Each session
shows the browser, operating system, IP address, sign-in time, and last activity.

To sign out one browser, click **Sign out** beside its session. To sign out every
browser except the one you are using, click **Sign out other sessions**.

A browser that is signed out must sign in again to use Virtool.

## Settings

Go to **Settings** to change how Virtool behaves for your account.

### Times

![Time format setting with Date and time selected](./time_format.png?url)

- **Time format**: **Relative**, the default, shows times such as _3 days ago_. **Date
  and time** shows times in your time zone, such as _Today at 11:12_, _Yesterday at
  09:30_, or _Sep 26 at 14:03_. Times from an earlier year show the date only, such as
  _Sep 26, 2023_.

Hover over a time or click it to see more. With **Relative**, you see the exact date and
time, such as _2023-08-14 09:31_. With **Date and time**, you see how long ago it was.

### Pathoscope export

These settings change how Pathoscope results are copied and downloaded.

- **Prefer acronym**: name OTUs by their acronym when they have one.
- **Column order**: drag columns to change their order. Drag a column below the **Hidden
  columns** line to leave it out.

### Reference exports

These settings are the defaults for OTU and isolate exports. You can change them in the
export menu for one export.

- **FASTA header**: **Accession**, or **Accession, OTU, and isolate**.
- **Table download format**: **CSV** or **TSV**.

## API keys

Use an API key to access the [Virtool API](/docs/api/uploads). The API currently
supports file uploads.

Each API key belongs to one account. Go to **API Keys** to manage your keys.

### Create an API key

1. Click **Create**.
2. Type a name for the key and select its permissions.

   You can't grant the key permissions that your account doesn't have. If permissions
   are removed from your account after the key is created, they're also removed from the
   key.

   Permissions limit only what the key can change. The key can read all the data that
   your account can read.

3. Click **Save**.
4. Copy the key, then click **Done**.

   The key is shown only once. You **can't** view the key value after you close the
   dialog. Keep it somewhere safe.

### Use an API key

Use HTTP Basic authentication. Use your Virtool username as the username, and the API
key as the password. See [Uploads](/docs/api/uploads) for an example.

### Change or delete an API key

In your key list, click the edit icon to change the permissions of a key. You can't
change its name.

Click the delete icon to delete a key. Virtool deletes the key immediately and does not
ask you to confirm.
