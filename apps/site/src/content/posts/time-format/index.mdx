---
title: Time format
summary: Show the exact date and time of an item instead of how old it is.
type: release
pr: 4220
---

Virtool shows times relative to now. A time such as _3 years ago_ can be any day in a
12-month range. This makes it difficult to find a sample from a certain date.

You can now show times as a date and time instead:

| When         | Relative    | Date and time   |
| ------------ | ----------- | --------------- |
| Today        | 2 hours ago | Today at 11:12  |
| This year    | 6 weeks ago | Sep 26 at 14:03 |
| Earlier year | 3 years ago | Sep 26, 2023    |

Hover over a time or click it to see the exact date and time, or how long ago it was.

![Reference created "3 years ago" and the exact time "2023-08-14 09:31" shown on hover](./references_tooltip.png?url)

The setting applies only to your account, and **Relative** stays the default. Learn more
in the [account guide](/docs/guide/account#times).
