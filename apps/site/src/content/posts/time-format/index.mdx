---
title: Time format
summary: Show the exact date and time of an item instead of how old it is.
type: release
pr: 4220
---

Virtool shows times relative to now, such as "3 years ago". That can be any day in a
12-month range. This makes it difficult to find a sample from a certain date.

You can now show times as a date and time instead, such as "Today at 11:12" or "Sep 26
at 14:03". Times from an earlier year show the date only, such as "Sep 26, 2023". Hover
over a time or click it to see the exact date and time, or how long ago it was.

![Reference created "3 years ago" with a tooltip that shows "2023-08-14 09:31"](./references_tooltip.png?url)

The setting applies only to your account, and **Relative** stays the default. Learn more
in the [account guide](/docs/guide/account#times).
