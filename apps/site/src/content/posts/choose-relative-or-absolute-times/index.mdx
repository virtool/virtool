---
title: Choose relative or absolute times
summary:
  Virtool can now show the exact date and time of an item, not only how old it is.
type: release
pr: 4220
---

Virtool showed all times as relative times, such as "1 minute ago".

![Reference list that shows "igboyes created 1 minute ago"](./references_relative.png?url)

You can now show times as a date and time instead, such as "Today at 12:04". Select
**Date and time** in your account settings, and every time in Virtool changes to this
format.

![Reference list that shows "igboyes created today at 12:04"](./references_date_and_time.png?url)

To see a time in the other format, hover over it or click it.

The setting applies only to your account. **Relative** stays the default.

To change the setting, see [Times](/docs/guide/account#times) in the account guide.
